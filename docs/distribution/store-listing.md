## English Listing

### Name / Title
Instagram Checker - Who Does Not Follow You Back

### Short Description (max 100 characters)
Find out who does not follow you back on Instagram. 100% private, client-side, zero password needed.

### Category
- Primary: Social & Communication (or Social)
- Secondary: Productivity

### Detailed Description
Instagram Checker helps you find out who does not follow you back on Instagram quickly, safely, and with complete privacy.

Unlike other tools, Instagram Checker runs 100% client-side directly in your browser:
- No passwords or login credentials ever required
- Zero server database or external data storage
- Zero tracking or analytics

KEY FEATURES:
- Comprehensive breakdown: Not following back, Fans, Mutual, and 8 Meta connection categories
- Dedicated full browser tab experience (no small popup that closes accidentally)
- Cross-tab detection: automatically communicates with your active Instagram tab
- Direct unfollow shortcut: one-click to open profiles and track your unfollow progress
- Hide unfollowed filter to keep your list organized
- Export your results to clean CSV (protected against formula injection)
- Dual language support: English and Bahasa Indonesia

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Make sure you are logged in to instagram.com in your browser.
2. Click the Instagram Checker extension icon to open the full-page dashboard.
3. Click Check to start reading your followers and following lists.
4. Review your results and export CSV anytime.

PRIVACY & SECURITY:
Your credentials and data never leave your device. All calculations occur inside your browser using standard browser APIs.
